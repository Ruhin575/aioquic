Three-panel step chart showing observed adaptive packet threshold K increases over time for three preliminary Dynamic experiments under the moderate network profile. Each run starts at K=3. The qlogs contain 461, 458 and 490 k_adapted events respectively, but only 65, 59 and 64 actual increases. No k_decayed events were recorded. The chart therefore shows observed increases only and does not represent a complete K trajectory or unlogged decay.
